How it works

  1. Describe the food

    The assistant reads quantity, type and allergens, and sets a safe-by time.

  2. Get matched

    NGOs see listings ranked by distance, timing, capacity and food type.

  3. Confirm the handover

    The donor approves or declines each request. Once approved, a volunteer takes the pickup.

  4. Meals delivered

    Only confirmed deliveries count toward the impact numbers.
